RISK DISCLOSURE & TERMS
Product terms model · September 2026

The short version: NexTrade is designed as a managed yield platform where capital is allocated across defined strategy pools and fees are earned from positive performance.

Returns are projected, not guaranteed. This public portfolio build demonstrates the intended product architecture and business model; live operation would require jurisdiction-specific legal, custody, compliance, and security approval.

The value of your balance may rise or fall based on Bitcoin market conditions and currency exchange movements.

NexTrade does not charge fees on deposited principal. Fees are earned only from profits generated by the platform.

NexTrade is not a savings account, bank deposit, or capital-guaranteed product.

1. Who Can Use NexTrade

You must be at least 18 years old and legally permitted to trade financial instruments in your jurisdiction. By creating an account, you confirm this. We reserve the right to close accounts that do not meet eligibility requirements.

2. Your Account

You are solely responsible for all activity under your account. Keep your credentials private. One person, one account — no sharing, no transfers to other users. We will never ask for your password via email, chat, or any other channel.

3. Deposits & Withdrawals

Deposits are subject to verification (1–5 business days). Withdrawals go only to verified methods on your account. NexTrade is not a bank. Balances are not covered by any deposit insurance scheme (e.g., FDIC, FSCS). Crypto assets are not SIPC-protected.

4. Investment & Pool Risk

Pool strategies are not savings accounts. Projected returns (22% per 90-day cycle for Steady Accumulator; 67% per 30-day cycle for Surge Pool) are gross strategy targets defined by the product model — they are not guarantees and actual pool performance may be lower or negative. Markets move against positions. You can lose part or all of your invested capital, particularly in high-risk strategies.

5. Performance Fee

NexTrade charges a performance fee of 15% (Steady Accumulator) or 20% (Surge Pool) on the profit generated by the pool during your investment term. This fee is deducted at the point of claim. Your principal is never subject to fees. If the pool generates zero profit, no fee is charged.

6. Early Exit

Withdrawing before a strategy's lock period expires incurs an early exit penalty of 8% (Steady Accumulator) or 15% (Surge Pool) of the total claimed value. This penalty is disclosed clearly before you confirm any early withdrawal.

7. Prohibited Activity

Strictly prohibited: money laundering, fraud, market manipulation, account sharing, scraping, or the use of automated bots without written consent. Violations result in immediate account termination and may be referred to relevant authorities.

8. Our Liability

NexTrade is not liable for losses arising from market movements, your investment decisions, blockchain outages, third-party failures, or your failure to secure your account. Our total liability in any circumstance is capped at the fees you paid to NexTrade in the preceding 12 months.

9. Privacy

NexTrade is designed not to sell personal data. A production operation would share data only with service providers required to run the platform under appropriate contractual and regulatory controls, and with authorities where legally required.

10. Disputes & Governing Law

Disputes are subject to binding arbitration under AAA rules, seated in Delaware. You waive the right to a class action and jury trial. Contact support first — we will make every reasonable effort to resolve disputes informally within 30 days. Governing law: State of Delaware, USA.
